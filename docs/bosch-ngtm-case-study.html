Overview

Making Supplier Onboarding Legible: A Route-Management ecosystem for Bosch NGTM

The Problem

Bosch's supplier onboarding ran across siloed workflows — manual handoffs, constant follow-ups, and Excel files between teams. Every stalled onboarding slowed route enablement and raised cost. It showed up on both sides:

  • Suppliers found it complex and unclear — many stopped responding.

  • Internal teams ran reactive — no escalation, no feedback data, high cognitive load, and no way to measure the delays.

Slow, error-prone, and invisible — a process nobody could see, and nobody could fix.

Scope

While the immediate scope is supplier onboarding, the findings and insights from this foundational study have broader implications across the other stakeholder management areas (Plant Onboarding and LSP onboarding).
