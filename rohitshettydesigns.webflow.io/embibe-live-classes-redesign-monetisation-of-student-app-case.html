Executive Summary

Problem

The Embibe Live Classes feature faced significant challenges in delivering value to users due to:

  • Fragmented registration processes hindered enrollment.

  • Low engagement with pre- and post-class activities.

  • Challenges in managing multiple class enrollments effectively.

  • Close stakeholder collaboration ensured alignment with goals.

PRoblem Statement

What was i Solving?

The Live Classes feature on Embibe struggles to deliver its intended value due to fragmented registration processes and low completion rates for Pre- and Post-Class activities. Users face challenges enrolling in multiple classes and engaging with class materials effectively. How might we simplify the registration process and enhance engagement with Pre- and Post-Class activities to maximize the learning experience and drive continued participation.
