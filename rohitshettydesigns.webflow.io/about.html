My Story

My journey has been fueled by curiosity, starting from school days when I built DIY experiments—including a one-seater electric car made entirely from scrap materials. Though it failed, it sparked my passion for problem-solving and innovation. This curiosity led me to pursue Mechanical Engineering, which taught me technical rigor but didn’t fully align with my creative aspirations.

After graduating, I began my career as a developer at TCS, where I built business intelligence dashboards. However, my passion for design pushed me to quit my job and dive into UX—a field that perfectly blended creativity with analytical thinking. Despite early challenges and failures, I landed roles at Siemens, Dell, and Embibe, where I led impactful projects like increasing user sign-ups by 64% and driving ₹1Cr quarterly revenue.

Along the way, I briefly pursued an M.A. in Counselling Psychology out of personal interest but discontinued it to fully commit to my Master of Design program at IIT Guwahati. This academic pursuit complements my professional expertise by deepening my understanding of human-computer interaction, systems thinking, and strategic design management.

Today, I specialize in creating scalable design systems and user-centered solutions that bridge user needs with business goals. Let’s connect!
