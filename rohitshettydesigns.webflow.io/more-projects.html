problem

Only onsite configuration was possible

Before the introduction of the SICAM Localizer, fault localization in power grids was more challenging and less efficient Traditional methods.

Onsite Configuration: Time-consuming and error-prone, especially in remote areas, leading to setup delays and potential issues.

Manual Inspections: Labor-intensive and prone to human error, causing delays in fault detection and resolution.

Less Sophisticated Monitoring Systems: Lack of real-time data and automated alerts, resulting in longer downtimes and higher maintenance costs.

Dashboard & Asset details

Fault-Prone Zones are areas in the power grid more susceptible to issues due to factors like extreme weather, aging infrastructure, and high demand. The

‍Asset Details Page provides comprehensive information on each asset, including identification, real-time condition monitoring, maintenance history, and lifecycle details, enabling efficient management and reduced downtime.

Asset Configuration

FSI (Fault Sensor Indicator) is an integral part of the SICAM Localizer system. It detects phase and ground faults on medium-voltage overhead lines. The FSI indicates faults via LED flashing or communicates fault information through the SICAM Fault Collector Gateway (FCG).
FPI (Fault Passage Indicator) configuration involves installing devices on overhead lines or substations to detect faults, with options for local or remote communication and adjustable sensitivity settings.
